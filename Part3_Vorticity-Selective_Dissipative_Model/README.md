## Key Results

- **Global Well-Posedness at Fixed Filter Scale:** For every fixed regularization scale $\ell > 0$, the $\mathrm{NS}_{\mathrm{TZT}}$ system on the periodic flat torus $\mathbb{T}^3$ possesses a unique global strong solution for arbitrary initial data $u_0 \in H^1_\sigma(\mathbb{T}^3)$ and localized external forcing.
- **Enstrophy Boundedness without Blow-up:** The cubic vortex stretching term is analytically dominated and absorbed by the cubic $L^3$ dissipation generated by the convex scalar potential structure of $F_{\mathrm{diss}}$, guaranteeing finite global enstrophy for all $t \ge 0$.
- **Elimination of Laminar Over-Damping:** In pseudo-spectral simulations of the Taylor–Green vortex ($Re=1600$, $N=64^3$), the model suppresses **over 80%** ($80.84\%$) of artificial dissipation during the early laminar stage compared to the standard Smagorinsky closure ($0.45 \times 10^{-4}$ vs $2.33 \times 10^{-4}$).

## The Mathematical Model: $\mathrm{NS}_{\mathrm{TZT}}$

The $\mathrm{NS}_{\mathrm{TZT}}$ system augments the incompressible Navier–Stokes equations on $\mathbb{T}^3 = [0, 2\pi)^3$:

$$\partial_t u + (u \cdot \nabla)u + \nabla p = \nu \Delta u + F_{\mathrm{diss}} + F_{\mathrm{axis}} + f, \qquad \nabla \cdot u = 0.$$

### 1. Rotational Selective Dissipation Operator
$$F_{\mathrm{diss}} = -\nabla \times \bigl(\nu_{\mathrm{TZT}}(\vert{}\omega\vert{})\,\omega\bigr), \qquad \nu_{\mathrm{TZT}}(s) = C_O \ell^2 s \, \Phi(s/\Omega_c), \qquad \omega = \nabla \times u.$$

### 2. Leray-Projected Gyroscopic Axis Term
$$F_{\mathrm{axis}} = c_X \Omega_c \mathbb{P} G(u, \omega), \qquad G(u, \omega) = \Phi(\vert{}\omega\vert{}/\Omega_c) \left(u \times \hat{\omega}_\varepsilon\right), \qquad \hat{\omega}_\varepsilon = \frac{\omega}{\sqrt{\vert{}\omega\vert{}^2 + \varepsilon^2}}.$$

| Component | Energy Budget | Mathematical & Physical Role |
|---|---|---|
| $F_{\mathrm{diss}}$ | Dissipates $C_O \ell^2 \int_{\mathbb{T}^3} \Phi \, \vert{}\omega\vert{}^3 \, dx \ge 0$ | Gradient of a convex potential (monotone); inactive in laminar regions ($\vert{}\omega\vert{} < \Omega_c$). |
| $F_{\mathrm{axis}}$ | Strictly Neutral: $\int_{\mathbb{T}^3} F_{\mathrm{axis}} \cdot u \, dx \equiv 0$ | Divergence-free, first-order gyroscopic alignment; stabilizes enstrophy without energy drain. |

## Scope and Boundary Conditions

* All mathematical statements concern the regularized dynamical system at a fixed filter scale $\ell > 0$. The analytical bounds depend on $(C_O \ell^2)^{-1}$ and degenerate in the limit $\ell \to 0$; no assertions are made regarding the classical $\ell=0$ Navier–Stokes Millennium problem.
* The theorem establishes global strong well-posedness in $H^1_\sigma(\mathbb{T}^3)$.
* Energy neutrality of $F_{\mathrm{axis}}$ holds on divergence-free vector fields with vanishing mean momentum.
